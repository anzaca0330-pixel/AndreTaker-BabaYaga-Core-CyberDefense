# FUNDING, SUPPORT & RESOURCE SPONSORSHIP
## Ecosistema Pericial AndreTaker / BaBaYaga Core

The **AndreTaker / BaBaYaga Core** ecosystem builds open-source digital forensic verification tools, cryptographic custody systems (ISO 27037), defensive cyber-resilience frameworks, and gamified education (*Guardianes Digitales*) for human rights defenders, legal advocates, youth, and independent auditors.

---

### 1. WHY SPONSOR OUR WORK?
Our tools were forged under extreme, real-world conditions: preserving **over 147,000 documents (>677 GB vault)** and verifying evidence integrity under active cyber-siege and exile.

By supporting this project, you fund:
1. **Free & Open-Source Tools:** Keeping our core forensic parsers (`andretaker-forensics`, `andretaker-core`) 100% open, accessible, and community-audited.
2. **Human Rights Legal Protection:** Enabling civil society organizations to generate cryptographically signed legal exhibits for international courts (IACHR / CIDH).
3. **Reproducible Science:** Providing open mathematical implementations of Second-Digit Benford's Law (2BL), `/FlateDecode` stream verification, and PDF XREF delta detection.
4. **Gamified Educational Empowerment:** Training the next generation of defenders, youth, and families through interactive RPG cyberdefense (*Guardianes Digitales*).

### 4. HOW FUNDERS WILL MEASURE SUCCESS (METRICS & IMPACT)

Funders and partners evaluate project success through clear, empirical key performance indicators (KPIs):

1. **Adoption & Usage:** Monthly active users of community CLI/GUI tools and downloads of the *Guardianes Digitales* educational module.
2. **Verification Integrity:** 100% test coverage and zero cryptographic collisions in the SHA-256 custody pipeline.
3. **Court Admissibility:** Successful filings of reproducible evidence packages adhering to ISO/IEC 27037 standards in international forums.
4. **Educational Reach:** Number of students, journalists, and activists trained through the interactive sandbox and educational guides.
5. **Transparency:** Public release of third-party security audit reports and fully documented GitHub milestone roadmaps.
