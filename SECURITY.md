### 3. REPORTING A VULNERABILITY
We welcome independent security audits, academic peer reviews, and responsible vulnerability reports from the global cybersecurity community.

#### Reporting Protocol:
1. **DO NOT file public GitHub Issues** for security-sensitive vulnerabilities, zero-day exploits, or data leakage bugs.
2. Send an encrypted email directly to: **`andretaker@proton.me`**.
3. **Subject Line Format:** `[SECURITY DISCLOSURE] - <Component Name> - <Brief Issue Summary>`.
4. **Include in your report:**
   * Affected repository, branch, and commit hash.
   * Step-by-step reproduction steps or Proof of Concept (PoC) under isolated sandbox conditions.
   * Potential impact on evidence integrity, hash calculation, or user privacy.
   * Your preferred name/pseudonym for public credit and acknowledgments.

---

### 4. OUR COMMITMENT & SLA
* **Initial Response & Triage:** Within **48 hours** of report receipt.
* **Assessment & Impact Verification:** Within **5 business days**.
* **Remediation & Patch Release:** Critical vulnerabilities affecting cryptographic hashing, evidence immutability, or local execution security will be patched and released within **14 business days**.
* **Public Coordinated Disclosure:** We will coordinate a joint public disclosure timeline with the reporter once the patch is published.

---

### 5. SAFE HARBOR & BUG BOUNTY ETHICS
Activities conducted in good faith, respecting the safety of human rights data, avoiding denial of service against live forensic vaults, and adhering to responsible disclosure guidelines will be treated as authorized security research. We will not pursue legal action against ethical researchers acting within these boundaries.
